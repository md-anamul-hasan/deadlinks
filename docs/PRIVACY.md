# DeadLinks privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

DeadLinks runs entirely on your PC. It makes no network connections: no telemetry, no update check, nothing is uploaded. It reads shortcut files in the folders you scan, and when you remove shortcuts it stores a copy, a manifest and a log (`removal-log.csv`) locally so removals can be restored.

There is no account, no licence key, no analytics and no crash reporting. Logs stay on your machine in `%APPDATA%\DeadLinks`.
